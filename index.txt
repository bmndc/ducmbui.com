how do I pronounce your name?
-----------------------------

In Vietnamese naming customs, the surname is positioned before the given name*,
and people are referred to by their first names. My full name is Bui Minh Duc,
thus I am called Duc, not Bui.

Phonetic breakdown of my first name:

- the Đ sounds like the standard English D sound;
- the ư is an unrounded vowel, spoken with a slight smile and spreaded lips;
- the c is unreleased and sounds like it ends in a sharp stop; you prepare to
  make a k sound but don't actually puff air out.

For English speakers, Duc rhymes with "book" or "look" but with d as the first
letter (dook). To mimic the rising tone, say as if you're asking a quick
question (dook?).

I'm okay with variations of my name such as _duck_ or _zook_, however.

*In legal papers where the given name is positioned first, I prefer writing my
name as "Duc Minh Bui".

[Vietnamese naming customs]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnamese_name
